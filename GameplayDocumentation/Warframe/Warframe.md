**Resolution Setup**

Change the monitor resolution, launch the game and open the video settings.

Verify that the selected resolution appears in the Resolution option, then change the game resolution to the right one. If the resolution is not available:

\- Change the Aspect Ratio setting.

\- Check which aspect ratio matches the resolution being tested.

Test the game in fullscreen or windowed borderless.

**Testing Guidelines**

Observation: If you don't have an account yet, start the game from the very beginning (character creation/tutorial). This makes it much easier to trigger every meter along the way.

1\. Enter a combat zone (any mission with active enemies) to trigger the healthBlue, inGameRed and inGameNotRed meters.
![healthBlue](images/healthBlue.png)
![inGameRed](images/inGameRed.png)

2\. Shoot or melee a living enemy until it dies to trigger the enemyDeadYellow, enemyDeadNotYellow, enemyDeadRed and enemyDeadNotRed meters.
![enemyDeadYellow](images/enemyDeadYellow.png)
![enemyDeadNotYellow](images/enemyDeadNotYellow.png)

3\. Completing any general task or achievement triggers a popup notification saying the task was completed, activating the taskBlue and taskWhite meters.
![taskBlue](images/taskBlue.png)
![taskWhite](images/taskWhite.png)

4\. Complete all of a mission's objectives and extract to trigger the missionCompleteBege1, missionCompleteBege2 and missionCompleteNotBege meters.
![missionCompleteBege1](images/missionCompleteBege1.png)

5\. Aboard your ship (Orbiter), open the star map to trigger the mapBege1, mapBege2 and mapNotBege meters.
![mapBege1](images/mapBege1.png)

6\. After completing all mission objectives, a green icon appears in the top right corner indicating extraction is ready, triggering the extractionGreen and extractionNotGreen meters.
![extractionGreen](images/extractionGreen.png)

7\. If you take poison-type damage, your health bar turns purple to indicate you are poisoned, triggering the poisonPurple meter.
![poisonPurple](images/poisonPurple.png)

8\. Engage a Boss to trigger the boss health meters, bossYellow and bossNotYellow.
![bossYellow](images/bossYellow.png)
![bossNotYellow](images/bossNotYellow.png)

9\. Upon dying, a yellow icon appears activating the death meter, triggering deathYellow and deathNotYellow.
![deathYellow](images/deathYellow.png)
![deathNotYellow](images/deathNotYellow.png)

10\. To get the Cipher meters, look for consoles with a hacking minigame. Interacting with them triggers the cipherYellow1, cipherYellow2 and cipherNotYellow meters.
![cipherYellow1](images/cipherYellow1.png)

11\. Some consoles run a blue variant of the hacking minigame instead of the yellow one, triggering the cipherBlue1, cipherBlue2 and cipherNotBlue meters. (Note: these three images are taken from the 3840x1080 resolution set, since no blue cipher variant exists in the 3840x2160 set.)
![cipherBlue1](images/cipherBlue1.png)
