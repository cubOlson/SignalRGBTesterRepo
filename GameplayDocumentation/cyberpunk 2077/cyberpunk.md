# Cyberpunk 2077

## Supported Resolutions

- 1920x1080
- 1920x1200
- 2560x1080
- 2560x1440
- 2560x1600
- 3440x1440
- 3840x1080
- 3840x2160
- 5120x1440
- 3840x2160 (HDR)

---

## Resolution Setup

Open the video settings.

Verify the following setting:

- **Window Mode:** Windowed Fullscreen

Also verify that the selected resolution is correct.

This allows the integration to properly adapt to all supported resolutions.

## Testing Guidelines

1. Launch the game and open:
   - **Load Game**

2. Select the save:
   - **Practice Makes Perfect**

3. Select the:
   - **Hacking Practice**

4. Immediately after entering the tutorial, the Progress Bar meters will trigger automatically.
![Progress Bar Meter](images/progressbar-meter.png)

5. Continue through the first tutorial section to trigger the Scan meters.
![Scan Meter](images/scan-meter.png)

6. During the crouch tutorial section, press:
   - **C**

   This triggers the following meters:
   - **Crouch**
   - **Car Red**
   - **Phone Red**

![Crouch Meter](images/crouch-meter.png)

7. Perform the first stealth takedown to trigger:
   - **SneakKill 1** meters
![SneakKill 1 Meter](images/sneakkill1-meter.png)

8. Continue to the next stealth section and perform the second stealth takedown to trigger:
   - **SneakKill 2**
   - **NotBlue**
![SneakKill 2 Meter](images/sneakkill2-meter.png)

9. Continue through the remaining tutorial sections until reaching the hacking sequence.

10. Complete the hacking tutorial to trigger:
   - **EncryptGreen**
   - **EncryptRed**
   - **EncryptYellow**
   - **NotYellow**
![Encrypt Meter](images/encrypt-meter.png)

11. After completing the tutorial, open:
   - **Load Game**

12. Select the save:
   - **Nocturne OP55N1**

13. After loading into the game, the following meters will trigger automatically:
   - **In Game Car Blue**
   - **Phone Blue**
   - **Map Blue**
![Blue Meter](images/blue-meter.png)

14. Press:
   - **T**

15. Open:
   - **Contacts**

16. Press:
   - **F**

   This triggers the Call meters.
![Call Meter](images/call-meter.png)

17. Open the map and locate the:
   - **Kiss icon**

18. Travel to the location and interact with the NPC, to get the joytoy meters.

   This triggers:
   - **mJTRedAct1**
   - **mFJTYellowAct1**
   - **fJTRedAct1**
![Joytoy Meter](images/joytoy-meter.png)

19. Open the map again and locate the nearest:
   - **Drink icon**

20. Enter the bar to trigger:
   - **Safe Green**
   - **Map Green**
![Safe Zone Meter](images/safezone-meter.png)

21. Press:
   - **V**

22. Call a vehicle and drive for a short period to trigger the Vehicle meters.
![Vehicle Meter](images/vehicle-meter.png)

23. Locate nearby police NPCs and attack them.

   This triggers:
   - **Map Red**
   - **Combat**
   - **Health**
   - **Wanted**
![Combat Meter](images/combat-meter.png)

24. Allow the police to kill the player in order to trigger the Dead meters.
![Dead Meter](images/dead-meter.png)
