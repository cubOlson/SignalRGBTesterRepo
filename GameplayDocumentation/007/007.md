## Resolution Setup

Open the video settings.

Verify the resolution on your monitor is the same as the resolution in the game.

## Testing Guidelines

1. Launch the game and film the mission:
   - The Needle's Eye: Going In
   - Complete the mission until you have the flag and have escaped in a boat

2. Film the mission:
    - A Night Out - until the hack is complete in the club 

3. In Game triggers - watch should be visible at bottom left, energy bar must be present to the left of watch
    - battery grey
    - right above the battery not grey
    - last blue energy meter (on watch, bottom left)
    - last green chemical meter
    ![In Game](images/inGame.png)

4. Confront Effect - 007 has been caught and is in dialogue
    - the orange lightning bolt
    - right next to the bolt, not orange
    ![Confront](images/confronted.png)

5. Alert Effects - triangular alert UI at top of screen and red, green, or yellow bands of color at top of screen
    - white meter in the triangle
    - red / green / yellow meters at the top of the screen, in the colorful section
    ![Alert - Trespass](images/trespass.png)
    ![Alert - Contained](images/contained.png)
    ![Alert - Danger](images/danger.png)

6. Energy Gained - usually in a fight, getting a hit in will gain you an energy point
    - orange lightning bolt
    - black box around it
    ![Energy Gained](images/energyGain.png)

7. Failed Mission - failed screen after death
    - red area above yellow letters
    - middle of yellow letters, wide and very short
    ![Mission Failed](images/failed.png)

8. Hacking Effects - watch turns to map, hacking UI visible
    - white and not white meters on bottom rectangle of download symbol
    - first square of the load bar will be cyan, red, or white depending on state. Three meters there
    ![Hacking](images/hack.png)

9. Low Energy - blue meter in watch
    - one meter just to the left of the top of the blue circular UI

10. Low Chemical - green meter in watch
    - one meter just to the left of the top of the green circular UI

11. Scan - 007 opens the scan menu which has up to 4 abilities in it
    - 2 grey / white meters on the interior grey squares with numbers / button names in them
    ![Scan](images/scan.png)

Possible effects for the future - hidden, reloading, got a kill. I only played 2-3 missions so any new thing after that also
