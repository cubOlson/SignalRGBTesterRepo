## Testing Guidelines

1. Launch the game and select **New Game**. Make sure you are playing survival.

2. Wake up from the pod. Open your meanu and let it load. Talk to the NOA (floating orb) and exhaust the dialogue.

3. Walk to the door marked "Adaptation". Touch the pink alien thing to the right and get your green Adaptation trigger.
![Adaptation](images/Adaptation.png)

4. Walk through the second door, hit the button and jump in the water. Get your High Pressure trigger here, red around the O2 meter plus the white arrow.

5. Swim to the other side of the gap. Talk to the NOA and exhaust dialogue.

6. Head out the door you came in and swim to the other side of the column. Flip 2 orange switches and swim back to the NOA, Hit launch.
![Switches](images/Switches.png)

7. Finish cutscene. When you load in, look at the Fabricator in the life pod. This is the Fabricator trigger.
![Fabricator](images/Fabricator.png)

8. Track the recipes for Survival Multitool and the Scanner.

9. Hop into the water and swim down. You will see titanium scattered around for the multitool. There will be copper and the pink stuff necessary for the scanner in the hole beneath the lifepod. This will provide all triggers for Health, Air, Water, Hunger, and the in-game condition. Low resource is 1/3 of the circle left. The in game meters are the orange and cyan element underneath the compass at the top of the screen.
![InGame](images/InGame.png)
![PinkThing](images/PinkThing.png)

10. Once you have built the scanner, you will get the databank trigger on the left side of the screen.
![DatabankAndPDA](images/DatabankAndPDA.png)

11. Swim to the welcome center on your map. It is a submerged building with a NOA inside, very close to the lifepod. You need to make a battery and insert it in the red receptacle here to turn on the biolab.
![WelcomeCenter](images/WelcomeCenter.png)
![BatteryHole](images/BatteryHole.png)

12. Once you have talked to the NOA, swim back to the entrance where the Biolab is. Use it and set your passive effects. This is the Biolab trigger.
![Biolab](images/Biolab.png)

13. Swim back to the lifepod and wait for the 10-11 minute mark while switching your tools around. This will trigger the "return to Lifepod" UI which is the trigger for the signal effect. 
![NOASignal](images/NOASignal.png)

14. Speak to NOA and choose the dialogue where he tells you to go die. There is an electric plant beneath the lifepod that will kill you if you swim into it enough. Get the "Player Died" trigger here.
![Dead](images/Dead.png)

14. After you have revived, trigger the Drowning effect by waiting at the bottom of the lifepod ladder, under the water, until the O2 meter is flashing red and the screen is black.
![Drowning](images/Drowning.png)

15. The PDA effect is triggered by the written message box you get at the bottom of the screen during dialogue.
