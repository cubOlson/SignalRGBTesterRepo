**Resolution Setup**

Change the monitor resolution, launch the game and open the video settings.

Verify that the selected resolution appears in the Resolution option, then change the game resolution for the right one. If the resolution is not available:

\- Change the Aspect Ratio setting.

\- Check which aspect ratio matches the resolution being tested.

Test the game in fullscreen or windowed borderless.

**Testing Guidelines**

1\. Launch the game and select a match as a Survivor. Pick any character and any perk.

2\. As soon as the match starts, you already get the inGameNotWhite, inGameWhite1 and inGameWhite2 meters.  
![inGameWhite1](images/inGameWhite1.png)

3\. Look for a generator and start repairing it. Once you start repairing, a yellow bar will appear slightly below the middle of the screen — this gives you the actionBar, actionWhite, actionNotWhite and actionColor meters. (These meters can also be triggered by other actions, such as healing an ally or attempting to escape a hook.)  
![actionBar](images/actionBar.png)

4\. While repairing a generator, a Skill Check will occasionally appear. As soon as it shows up, you'll get the skillCheck1, skillCheck2 and skillCheckBlack meters.  
![skillCheck1](images/skillCheck1.png)

5\. Hitting the Skill Check, or performing another action such as healing or repairing a generator alongside another player, grants Blood Points, which appear in the top-right corner of the screen. As soon as they appear, you'll get the bloodPointsRed, bloodPointsRed2 and bloodPointsWhite meters.  
![bloodPointsRed](images/bloodPointsRed.png)

6\. To get the playerHooked meter, let the Killer down you and wait for them to place you on a hook. Once you're on the hook, you'll get the playerHookedWhite, playerHookedRed and playerHookedRed2 meters.  
![playerHookedWhite](images/playerHookedWhite.png)

7\. Once you repair enough generators and open an exit gate, a bar will appear at the top of the screen — this is the endGameRed, endGameRed2, endGameColor and endGameGrey meter.  
![endGameRed](images/endGameRed.png)
