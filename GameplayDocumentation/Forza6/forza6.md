# Forza 6

## Supported Resolutions

- 1920x1080
- 1920x1200
- 2560x1080
- 2560x1440
- 2560x1600
- 3440x1440
- 3840x1080
- 3840x2160
- 5120x1440
- 3840x2160 (HDR / SDR)

---

## Resolution Setup

Open the video settings.

Verify that the selected resolution is correct in the game and on the monitor.

This allows the integration to properly adapt to all supported resolutions.

---

## Testing Guidelines

1. inGame trigger - one pink meter on the end of the pink section of the speedometer, one not pink meter next to it. One white meter on the arrow in the minimap.
![In Game](images/inGame.png)

2. Speed Effect triggers - one white meter on each of the numbers in the speed section, 3 total. One notwhite meter directly below the speed numbers. 

3. Map trigger - one white meter in the level number block, one not white meter next to it. One yellow meter in the "CR" money symbol.
![map](images/map.png)

4. ABS - blue "ABS" light near the speedometer

5. XP - white star with yellow xp arc
![ABS, XP](images/abs,xp.png)

6. Reverse - reverse "R" appears near speedometer
![Reverse](images/reverse.png)

7. Upshift - gear number and surrounding circle become pink, 2 pink meters
![upshift](images/upshift.png)

8. Winner - white meter in level box with not white meter next to it, one yellow meter in the yellow name
![winner](images/winner.png)

9. Wrong Way - yellow meter, black meter in the arrow symbol
![Wrong Way](images/wrongway.png)
