## Resolution Setup

Open the video settings.

Verify the resolution on your monitor is the same as the resolution in the game.

## Testing Guidelines

1. Launch the game. Enter a multiplayer lobby, do not host. Set possible regions to all possible regions.

2. Attempt to enter the game as a hider. This means not standing in the center during the lobby phase. It is okay if you are auto-selected, but you can only be a hider from the beginning.
    ![Avoid the hunter circle](images/hunterCircle.png)

3. Find a spot to hide. Be out in the open but paint and pose well. You need to score points before you are killed. 

4. Bury your model in the environment to see the buried effect.
    ![Buried Effect](images/buried.png)

4. Choose a pose to blend into the environment with R.
    ![Choose a good pose](images/poseMenu.png)

5. Colormatch yourself with paint mode, F. Pay attention to your reflectivity (Metallic) and matte level (Roughness).
    ![Paint yourself](images/paintMode.png)

6. When the round starts you will see the round effect, and the hourglass timer should begin.
    ![Round transition](images/roundStart.png)

7. Stay still until you score points, usually by being in someone's line of sight. Time pressure makes everyone bad at seeing.    
    ![Score points](images/hidingScore.png)

8. When your name is in the top 3 in the missed-spot ranking, you will get the rank 1, 2, or 3 effect.   
    ![Ranked effect](images/missedSpot.png)

9. Either die and become a hunter or start as a hunter next round. Make sure you get points to see the hunter score UI
    ![Hunter Score](images/hunterScore.png)
