**Resolution Setup**

Change the monitor resolution, launch the game and open the video settings.

Verify that the selected resolution appears in the Resolution option, the change de game resolution for the right one. If the resolution is not available:

\- Change the Aspect Ratio setting.

\- Check which aspect ratio matches the resolution being tested.

Test the game in fullscreen or windowed borderless.

**Testing Guidelines**

Observation: Since there is no game mode against bots, avoid submitting reports to avoid being punished.

1\. Launch the game and find a quick match, look for searchBlue meter.![searchBlue](images/searchBlue.png)

2\. When entering the match, check the inMatch, selecting and lobbyblack meters.  
![inMatch](images/inMatch.png)  
3\. Se você começar o round como atacante, verifique se os meters do Drone estão funcionando.  
[drone](images/drone.png)

4\. Ao começar o jogo, verifique o meter de arma principal selecionada selecionada no próprio signalRGB.  
5\. Look for a green Healthbar meter and check it.  
![health](images/health.png)  
6\. Move to a bomb site and plant the bomb to trigger the bomb-related meters.  
![BombPlanted](images/BombPlanted.png)

7\. During the round, trigger combat-related meters by:  
   \- Taking damage to activate Low Health meters.  
   \- Killing enemy players to activate Red and Blue Kill meters.  
![redkill1](images/redkill1.png)  
8.Use grenades to see the grenade effect.  
![granade](images/granade.png)  
9\. Fire repeatedly until you're almost out of ammo to activate the redammo effect.  
![lowAmmo](images/lowAmmo.png)  
10\. At the end of 2 turns you switch sides, from attacker to defender, check the Defendingblue meter in the Signal software.
