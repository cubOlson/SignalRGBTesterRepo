# Fortnite

## Supported Resolutions

- 1920x1080
- 1920x1200
- 2560x1080
- 2560x1440
- 3440x1440
- 3840x1080
- 3840x2160
- 5120x1440
- 3840x2160 (HDR)

---

## Resolution Setup

Open the video settings.

Verify the following setting:

- **Window Mode:** Fullscreen Windowed

This allows the integration to properly adapt to all supported resolutions.

---

## Keyboard Setup

Open the keyboard settings and assign a key for:

- **Sprint**

This is required in order to validate the Sprint meters during gameplay.

---

## Testing Guidelines

1. Launch the game and select **Battle Royale** mode.

2. During the pre-game waiting area, test the build-related meters by collecting wood materials.
![Build Meter](images/build-meter.png)

3. After the countdown ends, enter the Battle Bus phase to trigger the bus-related meters.
![Battle Bus Meter](images/battle-bus-meter.png)

4. Choose any location on the map and deploy.

5. After landing, several in-game and health-related meters will trigger automatically.

6. Search for loot chests and collect shield potions to trigger the Shield meters.
![Shield Meter](images/shield-meter.png)

7. Complete missions and gain XP during gameplay to trigger the XP meters.
![XP Meter](images/xp-meter.png)

8. Eliminate at least one enemy player to activate the Kill meters.
![Kill Meter](images/kill-meter.png)

9. Take damage intentionally to trigger:
   - **Low Health** effect
   - **Red Health** meters
![Low Health Meter](images/low-health-meter.png)
![Red Health Meter](images/red-health-meter.png)

10. Wait for the storm circle to begin closing to trigger the Circle Close meters.
![Circle Close Meter](images/circle-close-meter.png)

11. Find a vehicle, preferably near gas stations or roadside areas, and enter it to trigger the Vehicle meters.
![Vehicle Meter](images/vehicle-meter.png)

12. Use the assigned Sprint key to trigger the Sprint meters.
![Sprint Meter](images/sprint-meter.png)

13. Survive until the end of the match and win the game to trigger the Victory meters.
![Victory Meter](images/victory-meter.png)

14. To trigger the final Victory meter (**Win Orange**), you must achieve a **Crowned Victory Royale**.
    
    You can obtain a Crown by:
    
    - Finishing among the top players in a previous match
    - Eliminating another player who already has a Crown

    Crowned Victory Royales are currently available in:
    
    - Solo
    - Duos
    - Trios
    - Squads

![Crowned Victory Meter](images/crowned-victory-meter.png)
